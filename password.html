Keep your app logins together in an encrypted vault. Each app gets a simple monogram logo; nothing is loaded from third-party logo services.

◇
Encrypted on this device. Your vault is stored in this browser as AES-256-GCM ciphertext. Your master password is not stored. Export an encrypted backup to move it to another device. If you forget your master password, the vault cannot be recovered.

Encryption protects the stored vault, not a compromised device, keylogger, malicious browser extension, or someone using your unlocked session. The vault locks after five minutes without activity and when you switch away from the page.
